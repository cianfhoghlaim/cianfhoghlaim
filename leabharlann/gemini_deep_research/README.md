# Gemini Deep Research Archive

The PDF files contained within this directory and its subdirectories (culture, law, medical, politics, technology, etc.) have been generated using the official **Gemini Deep Research** feature available via [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).

## Methodology

These reports are the product of **informed and expert prompting** designed to leverage Gemini's agentic research capabilities. Unlike standard conversational AI outputs, these documents were created through an iterative, multi-step process:

1.  **Autonomous Planning**: Gemini generates a comprehensive research plan based on specialized prompts, identifying key areas of inquiry.
2.  **Iterative Reasoning & Verification**: The system identifies knowledge gaps, formulates targeted search queries, and verifies findings across hundreds of sources across the open web.
3.  **Multimodal Grounding**: Where applicable, proprietary documents and specific policy frameworks were provided to ground the research in factual, primary-source data.
4.  **Comprehensive Synthesis**: The final outputs are long-form reports with inline citations and structured analysis.
