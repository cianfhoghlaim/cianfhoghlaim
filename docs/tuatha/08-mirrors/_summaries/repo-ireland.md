# ireland — KCG Summary

## What It Is
A curated collection of historical and geographical map images of Ireland and Britain, gathered for the `tuatha/` educational MMO's Celtic-themed game world. Includes pre-Anglo-Norman school maps, early peoples and politics maps, provincial maps, and satellite imagery.

## Why This Matters for Kings' College Galway
These maps directly inform the MMO's spatial data layer — the game world geography, province boundaries, ancient school locations, and historical territorial divisions used for quest narratives. The satellite imagery of Great Britain and Ireland serves as reference for procedural terrain generation in the Babylon.js 3D environment.

## Contents
- `Provinces_of_Ireland_location_map.svg.png` — Modern provincial boundaries (SVG raster)
- `Pre_anglo_norma_invasion_irish_ancient_schools_map.jpg` — Pre-Norman monastic school locations for educational quest nodes
- `MODIS_-_Great_Britain_and_Ireland_-_2012-06-04_during_heat_wave.jpg` — NASA MODIS satellite imagery for terrain reference
- `Britain.Deisi.Laigin.jpg` — Historical territorial map of the Déisi and Laigin tribes
- `Ireland_early_peoples_and_politics.gif` — Early medieval peoples and political divisions
- `IrelandVIII-FirstThirdIX-es.svg.png` — 8th/9th century political boundaries
- `Northern_Ireland_-_Counties.png` — County map (modern administrative reference)
