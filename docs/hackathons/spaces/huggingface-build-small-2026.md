## Philosophy (Chapter Zero)

The hackathon aims to recapture the 2021-era AI tinkerer spirit — when models were small enough to run on a laptop and the vibe was fun and hopeful. Rather than building YAB2BSaaS with a giant LLM, builders are encouraged to **think small**: solve a real problem for someone they know, or build something whimsical and delightful.

## Two Main Tracks

### Track 1 — Backyard AI

Solve a real problem for someone you actually know (neighbor, parent, small-business owner). Build something that makes their day measurably better.

**Judging criteria:**
- Problem is specific and real
- The person actually used it
- Honest fit between problem and the small-model constraint
- Polish of the Gradio app

## Three Rules (Constraints)

1. **Small Models Only** — Total parameters ≤ 32 billion. Model fits on a laptop.
2. **Built on Gradio** — App must be a Gradio app, hosted as a Hugging Face Space.
3. **Show, Don't Tell** — Short demo video + social media post required for submission.
