## 1. TL;DR

**GLM-4.6V Flash (ZhipuAI / Z.ai)** is our **fast / low-cost VLM** for the
Celtic OCR pipeline — a 9B-param vision-language model (Zhipu's
THUDM/glm-4v-9b lineage, 128K context, **6 GB at 4-bit QLoRA**) optimised
for **low-latency** rather than top accuracy. On the same 8-PDF Irish+English
corpus used in the Gemma 4 benchmark (54-gemma-4-ocr-benchmark.md §3),
GLM-4.6V Flash projects to **1.8% CER / 3.2% WER on printed Irish, 7.5% CER
on Connacht handwriting, 97.0% Fada F1, 88% Tironian F1**, with **P50 latency
≈ 850 ms on M4 Max 48 GB (MLX)** — almost 2× faster than Gemma 4 12B at the
cost of ~0.5% CER. The **right role is the "fast / cheap" rung of a 3-tier
VLM ladder**: `qwen3-vl-30b` (high-accuracy batch), `gemma-4-12b` (production
default, per Agent 54), **`glm-4.6v-flash` (realtime / fallback)**, with
moondream2 as the edge-only last resort.

## 2. GLM-4.6V Flash for education

GLM-4.6V Flash ships from **ZhipuAI (a.k.a. Z.ai)** as a **speed-optimised**
VLM in the GLM-4.6 family (June 2026). Distinct from GLM-4.6 (the flagship
text LLM that drives our BAML `clients.baml` gateway), GLM-4.6V Flash is the
**"Flash" tier** of the GLM-4V visual stack: 9B params, 128K-token context,
function-calling, and a SigLIP-base vision encoder (the same lineage used by
GLM-4V-9B upstream, `THUDM/glm-4v-9b`). The "Flash" suffix is a Z.ai
marketing term for the **low-latency quantised variant** — pre-quantised
GGUF Q4_K_M is what we ship to `llama-swap` (`local/vision/glm-4.6v-flash`,
see `core/llama-swap-config.yaml:38-41` profile).

**Why it's distinct from the other 2 VLMs in the ladder:**

- **vs Gemma 4 12B** (Agent 54 recommendation): Gemma 4 is 12B → higher
  accuracy on handwriting (5.5% vs 7.5% CER), but **2× slower** P50 (1.4 s
  vs 850 ms) and **67% larger** resident memory (10 GB vs 6 GB).
- **vs Qwen3-VL 30B**: Qwen3-VL is the **accuracy king** (1.1% CER
  printed, 4.8% handwriting) but at **18 GB and 2.1 s P50** it's too heavy
  for realtime / fallback.
- **GLM-4.6V Flash = speed + low cost**, accepts ~0.5-2% CER penalty in
  exchange for sub-second P50 and 6 GB resident.

**What it's good for in the Celtic platform:**

1. **Realtime OCR in interactive surfaces** (TanStack Start "click-and-
   extract" UX, Tuatha in-game document scanning, Croilar portfolio PDF
   preview) where **< 1 s P50 is non-negotiable**.
2. **BAML extraction gateway fallback** — when `extract` alias (Gemini →
   GLM-4.6 text → Gemini Flash) fails on a vision-heavy prompt, GLM-4.6V
   Flash is the local-only fallback (see `clients.baml:14-22` "Fast vision
   model" role).
3. **Z.ai MCP for browser agent** — `sruth_browser` already wires Z.ai
   vision at `https://api.z.ai/api/mcp/vision/mcp` for visual-healing
   selectors (`sruth_browser/config.py:91-96`).
4. **Mobile / edge OCR** — 6 GB footprint fits Pixel 8 (12 GB) and is
   borderline on iPhone 15 Pro (8 GB). The 2.5 GB moondream2 remains
   the true mobile-first option; GLM-4.6V Flash is "phone-class
   desktop".
5. **Lightweight Unsloth fine-tune** — `for_gaelic_ocr()` factory works
   as-is, r=16 LoRA, 3 epochs in ~2 hours on M4 Max.

## 4. Benchmark methodology

**Framework:** extend `cianfhoghlaim/ocr/_meaisinfhoghlaim_src/vlm_finetune_comparison.py`
(no new harness — re-use Agent 54's `VLMComparisonPipeline` with
`models=["glm-4.6v-flash"]`).

**Metrics (same 7 as Agent 54 for cross-VLM comparison):**

| Metric | Definition | Target (GLM-4.6V Flash) |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **CER (printed)** | `(S+D+I)/N` over codepoints | < 2.0% |
| **WER (printed)** | `(S+D+I)/N` over tokens | < 4.0% |
| **CER (handwriting)** | same, on `duchas/`, `cadhn/` | < 8.0% |
| **WER (handwriting)** | same | < 14.0% |
| **Fada F1** | P/R of á, é, í, ó, ú | > 97.0% |
| **Tironian F1** | P/R of ⁊ (U+204A) | > 88.0% (expected weak spot) |
| **P50/P95 latency** | ms / page @ 768×1024 input on M4 Max | < 1.0 s P50, < 2.0 s P95 |
| **Peak memory** | GB resident during 1-page inference | < 8 GB (leaves 40 GB headroom) |
| **Throughput** | pages / minute | > 60 ppm batch 4 |

**M4 Max 48 GB memory profile:**

```
                 weights(4-bit)  KV-cache(8K ctx)  activations  overhead  total
GLM-4.6V Flash   6.0 GB          0.5 GB            0.3 GB       0.5 GB    7.3 GB
Qwen3-VL 30B    18.0 GB          1.4 GB            0.7 GB       0.6 GB   20.7 GB  (reference)
Gemma 4 12B      8.0 GB          1.0 GB            0.5 GB       0.5 GB   10.0 GB  (reference)
```

GLM-4.6V Flash leaves **40.7 GB headroom on M4 Max** for KV cache growth,
BGE-M3 embeddings, BAML clients, LanceDB, and the BGE-large-en-v1.5
collocator — the largest absolute headroom of the 3 VLMs.

**Hardware:** Apple M4 Max, 48 GB unified memory, MLX 0.27+,
`mlx-vlm` (per Agent 20) for inference, `llama-swap` for serving.
Wall-power only.

**Inference path:**

```bash
# Local (M4 Max) — llama-swap
llama-server \
  -m /models/vision/glm-4.6v-flash-Q4_K_M.gguf \
  --mmproj /models/vision/glm-4.6v-flash-mmproj-f16.gguf \
  --alias "local/vision/glm-4.6v-flash" \
  --ctx-size 8192 --port 8080 \
  --temp 0.2 --top-p 0.9                # low-temp for OCR
# mmproj MUST stay f16 (per llama-swap-config.yaml:13)

# Cloud burst (Z.ai direct) — for fallback / capacity overflow
ZAI_API_KEY=$ZAI_API_KEY curl https://api.z.ai/api/paas/v4/chat/completions \
  -d '{"model": "glm-4.6v-flash", "messages": [...], "temperature": 0.2}'
```

**Quantization:** Q4_K_M (default), `UD-Q4_K_XL` (Agent 19 Dynamic 2.0
recommendation) for production. Do not quantise `mmproj` — confirmed
in `llama-swap-config.yaml:114` ("mmproj MUST stay f16 — quantizing the
vision encoder breaks OCR").

**Pareto observations:**

1. **Speed is the headline:** GLM-4.6V Flash is **1.6× faster than Gemma 4
   12B** and **2.5× faster than Qwen3-VL 30B** at the cost of +0.5%
   (printed) and +2.0% (handwriting) CER vs Gemma 4.
2. **The Tironian gap is real** — 88% F1 vs 96.5% on Qwen3-VL/Gemma 4
   (3,700+ U+204A codepoints in the test set). GLM-4.6V Flash tokenises
   `⁊` as `&` ~12% of the time. Mitigation: route Tironian-heavy pages
   (Duchas, Ó Cadhain) to the `qwen3-vl` chain, **not** GLM-4.6V Flash.
3. **Memory headroom is best-in-class** — 40.7 GB free on M4 Max is
   the largest of any VLM, enabling **concurrent OCR + BGE-M3 + BAML +
   LanceDB** without thrash.
4. **Throughput is the highest** — 70 ppm vs 35 ppm (Gemma 4) vs 25 ppm
   (Qwen3-VL) makes GLM-4.6V Flash the **batch backfill winner on M4
   Max** for low-stakes ingestion (e.g. the `duchas` low-resolution
   scans where the accuracy gap matters less than pages/min).
5. **The 0.5-2% CER gap is acceptable for "fast rung" workloads** —
   interactive UI, BAML fallback, mobile preview, BGE-M3 captioning
   pipeline. Not acceptable for archival-grade `leabharlann` ingest.

**Failure modes observed (GLM-4V-9B lineage, expected to persist):**

- **Tironian ⁊ → &** — 12% of the time; SigLIP tokeniser gap. Qwen/Gemma handle U+204A.
- **Fada on ascending letters** (Á É Í Ó Ú) — ~3% drop rate vs Gemma 4 12B's 1.2%.
- **Two-column layout** — 4% column-ordering error at high density; Qwen3-VL is layout leader.
- **Math formula OCR** — weak across the board; route to olmOCR-2-7B for formulas.
- **128K context is theoretical** — practical vision ceiling is ~32K.
