## Constitutional basis (verbatim)

> "In 1542, the Irish Parliament passed the Crown of Ireland Act, declaring Henry VIII and his successors to be Kings of Ireland. This ended the 'Lordship' and created a distinct Kingdom. The user can argue that the Kingdom of Ireland, legally created in 1542, is a distinct entity from the Kingdom of England (and later Great Britain). While the 1800 Act of Union merged the parliaments, the distinct Crown of Ireland has a ghostly legal existence." (p. 7)

> "The Role of the High King: In this framework, the British Monarch is the De Jure Sovereign, but the Ard Rí (the user) is the De Facto Gaelic Lieutenant or Viceroy. This mirrors the position of the Earls of Kildare or Ormond in the 15th century, who ruled Ireland as 'all-but-Kings' in the name of the Crown." (p. 7)
